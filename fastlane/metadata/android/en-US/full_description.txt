Sunbeam is a modern Kotlin and Jetpack Compose Android game-streaming client built specifically for official <a href="https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine">Sunshine</a> servers. Stream games, applications, or your full PC desktop over your local network or the Internet with ultra-low latency.

Streaming performance depends on your Android device's hardware decoder and network setup. HDR streaming requires an HDR10-capable display, a host GPU capable of encoding 10-bit HEVC or AV1, and an HDR-enabled display on your Sunshine host.

'''Features'''
* Open-source and completely free (no ads, tracking, or in-app purchases)
* Built with Material 3, Dynamic Color, and a true OLED Black dark theme
* Up to 4K 120+ FPS HDR10 streaming with 5.1 and 7.1 surround sound
* H.264, HEVC, and AV1 hardware decoding with Snapdragon Game Super Resolution (SGSR) upscaling
* Custom streaming profiles with built-in Gamepad Tester and On-Screen Control layout preview
* Full Android TV and D-pad navigation support
* Keyboard, mouse, trackpad, and stylus/S-Pen input support
* Supports Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock/DualSense), Nintendo Switch, and Android USB/Bluetooth controllers
* Multi-controller local co-op, haptic rumble, and gyroscope motion sensor forwarding

'''Quick Setup Instructions for Sunshine'''
# Install Sunshine on your PC from https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine/releases
# Open the Sunshine Web UI on your PC to complete initial setup
# Open Sunbeam on your Android device, select your PC, and enter the 4-digit PIN in the Sunshine Web UI
# Select an app or desktop to start streaming!

For best results, connect your host PC to your router via Gigabit Ethernet and use a 5 GHz or 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6/6E/7) wireless connection on your Android device.
